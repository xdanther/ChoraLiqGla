Simple open-source Android TV app to stream music from your own Subsonic or Navidrome servers or local storage.

Chora has a simple, modern 10-foot UI built with Material 3 for Android TV, including artwork-coloured now-playing backgrounds and synced lyrics.

Important: Chora does not provide any music by itself. It's merely a front-end for Subsonic servers and/or local storage, meaning the end user has to provide the music files.
